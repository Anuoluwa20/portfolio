# UX profile — portfolio (ledger edition, Nov 2026)

## Product
A personal portfolio for a product and brand/packaging designer in Nigeria. Two audiences read it equally: people hiring for product roles, and clients commissioning brand, packaging and campaign work.

## Page patterns (marketing route types)
- `/` — **Home / Portfolio index**: TV intro (once per session) → title card hero (#home) → (01) Selected work: davemiye-style carousel of large rounded cards, snap + drag + arrows, "View project" pointer bubble → (02) More projects → (03) Playground: draggable pinboard (Shuffle / Tidy up), two columns on phones, native <dialog> viewer → (04) Services → (05) Skills → (06) Testimonials → (07) About → (08) Contact: chat (quick replies, three questions, opens a prefilled email; nothing is sent from the page) → footer. Nav: Home, Work, Playground, Services, Skills, About, Contact (menu button below 768px). Option A = black, list-style More projects and testimonial rows; Option B = white, picture grid and testimonial cards. `/archive` is a filtered picture grid (All / Mobile / Web) opening a slide-over; no Client labels anywhere.
- `/work/:slug` — **Case study**: nav → metadata + hero image → story sections → read next → footer. Product projects are text-led; brand and packaging projects are image-led.
- `/archive` — **Portfolio index**, table skin: 14 rows in an L1 frame; each row opens a slide-over quick look (Base UI Dialog, loaded on demand). No search or pagination: one short, fixed list.
- `*` — 404 (one message, one action).

## Aesthetic reference
Cinematic entrance, standard sections (owner's direction, 14 Nov 2026): a chapter-card intro, then numbered sections with one consistent head (hairline, number, title, one-line intro, control on the right). Black and white only, no colour accent: the work brings the colour. Project pages keep each project's own palette. No "Client" tags; non-client work still carries its status (Design challenge, Self-initiated).

## Type
- Bricolage Grotesque (variable: opsz 12–96, wdth 75–100, wght 200–800), chosen by the owner; carries the naira sign (U+20A6). Weights used: 400, 500, 700. Display uses opsz 96 and tight tracking; the title card is uppercase at line-height 0.95 (documented exception).
- IBM Plex Mono 400/500: labels, numbers, clock. Carries U+20A6.
- Sizes: body 1rem/1.55, secondary 0.875rem/1.5, meta 0.75rem/1.4. Headings on a Minor Third scale: 1.2rem (h3), 1.44rem (h2), display 1.728–5.16rem at 1.1; chapter titles up to 7.43rem and the clock up to 8.916rem (scale steps 11–12). Fluid clamps interpolate between steps. Weights 400 / 500 / 600 only.
- Exception: the footer wordmark is sized in `vw` so the name spans the page.
- Mono meta labels are set in capitals by CSS; their source text, buttons and links stay in sentence case.
- Icons are flex-centred on single-line boxes (re-check the optical offset if the face changes again).

## Colour (black and white only, chosen by the owner)
- Option A: ground #0A0A0A · text #F5F5F5 · rule #262626 · muted #8F8F8F (6.4:1) · L1 #141414
- Option B: ground #FFFFFF · text #0A0A0A · rule #E5E5E5 · muted #6B6B6B (5.3:1) · L1 #F5F5F5
- "accent" equals the text colour. Emphasis comes from weight and ink against grey (the hero sentence is grey, its two doors are full ink).
- Contact is the one inverted block (`.flip-block`, values spelled out; don't name a class `invert`, it is a Tailwind filter utility).
- Intro: the flash is the text colour; the sweep is a grey band that only dims the letters (multiply on black, screen on white).

## Motion — two registers
The owner asked for expressive motion (Nov 2026), so motion runs in two registers with one story: *a ledger that comes alive* — numbers count, entries get stamped, rows open into each project's world.

### Editorial register (documented exception to the 100–300ms tokens)
Hero, scroll reveals and project moments. Personality: confident ledger — strong ease-out (`--ease-mograph` cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1)), tight stagger, no overshoot. Crunch House alone is playful (`--spring-pop`, CSS `linear()` damped spring, bounce ≈ 0.13).
- Durations: 600 / 900 / 1200ms (`--ed-600/900/1200`); word stagger 32ms.
- Hero: words rise out of masks on first paint (pure CSS, no JS wait); work chips pop in at 650/800ms; meta line at 700ms; the index lifts in at 450ms (transform only, first load only, so LCP isn't delayed and the back-morph isn't disturbed).
- Scroll-driven (CSS `animation-timeline: view()`, compositor-only, no JS; content stays visible where unsupported): rows rise, statuses land as stamps, images wipe open behind a page-coloured shutter and settle from 1.12, display headings rise word by word, chapter titles drift sideways, the footer wordmark stretches from 62% to 100% width, a 2px progress hairline tracks reading.
- Index: preview image rises out of 6% with a settle (600ms); the counter rolls (NumberFlow).
- Routes: shared-element morph 600ms; the new page rises 32px while the old fades in 150ms.
- Moments: MONTRA counts in / out / kept and splits a ledger bar (beats 0 / 800 / 1500ms); Confam types a handle, sweeps four sources, then drops three sample posts (beats to 3.1s, replayable); Crunch floods the flavour colour out from the pressed button, drops the pack on a spring and throws six crisps; Explore Africa's sticky section rolls the clock 06:04 → 16:30 → 20:00 → LOS → DXB as the reader scrolls, with the ground following the time of day. Nothing autoplays on a loop.
- Only `transform` and `opacity` animate, except two contained text effects: the footer wordmark's `font-stretch` (one line, clipped container, no sibling reflow) and NumberFlow digits.

### Intro (TV channel-surfing, home only, once per session)
Beat sheet: a rounded TV screen in the middle of the page powers on (white line opens, 460ms) → static → seven channels, one project each (holds 420→130ms), each slides in with a sideways motion blur and leaves the same way, 50ms of static between, with a "CH 0n" on-screen label and the project name → last static → the screen clip opens to the whole window (650ms) while the ground fades to the page colour → the name rises word by word over the hero name (match cut), a grey band sweeps the letters → "Chapter 01 — Selected work, 2026" → dissolve. ≈4.5s. Static is a 240×160 canvas redrawn ~22 times a second. Skip with the button, any key or a click; never with reduced motion; inline failsafe at 3.6s if the app never loads.

### Chrome register
- Tokens: 100 / 150 / 200 / 300ms only. Enter `cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1)`; exit `cubic-bezier(0.7,0,0.84,0)`.
- Only `transform` and `opacity` animate. No bounce, no loops, no preloader, no hero text reveal.
- Signature: the index preview image morphs into the project hero (native View Transitions, `view-transition-name: project-<slug>`), 300ms ease-out; the rest of the page crossfades in 200ms. Back navigation reverses it.
- Filter: rows that stay slide to their new place with a 200ms transform (FLIP via the Web Animations API — no height animation); rows that arrive fade in over 200ms.
- Preview pane: image swap is an opacity crossfade, 150ms.
- Press feedback: `scale(0.98)` for 100ms.
- Archive slide-over: panel fades and moves 32px from the right in 200ms ease-out; exit 150ms ease-in. Tooltips fade in 150ms.

## Exceptions (documented)
- None to the duration tokens. The research suggested a 450–550ms morph; it is held to 300ms to stay inside the token set.
- The shared-element morph is the browser's View Transition: its `::view-transition-group` pseudo-element animates size and position of a snapshot. No DOM layout property animates, and nothing reflows.

## Reduced motion
`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` → every editorial animation is off and content shows in its final state; view transitions are instant; the filter's FLIP is skipped; moments jump to their last beat (numbers, posts, bars); the clock story still steps with scroll but swaps in 150ms fades; press scale, hover nudges and the pack drop are removed; the slide-over fades only.

## Viewports
Tokens only: narrow (< 768px), medium (≥ 768px), wide (1280px, `--vp-wide` caps main content; header and footer run full width). Narrow: the index is stacked cards, the filter is a three-segment control, no preview pane, header shows Work · About · Contact (Archive is linked from the index and footer). Hit targets 44×44 (WCAG 2.1 AA).
